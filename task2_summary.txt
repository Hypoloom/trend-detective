# Task 2: Sub-monthly temperature (night-time temperature and chill hours)

Pilot is monthly, so the hourly/daily timing runs were postponed. Existence of products was checked, and a monthly day/night product was fetched.

## Findings (VERIFIED, run locally unless noted)

| Product | Exists | Variables | File size (1 file, whole globe) |
|---|---|---|---|
| M2T1NXSLV (hourly) | Yes | T2M | 394 MB per day |
| M2SDNXSLV (daily stats) | Yes | T2M, T2MMAX, T2MMIN, T2MMEAN | 1.57 MB per day |
| M2SMNXSLV (monthly stats) | Yes | T2M, T2MMAX, T2MMIN, T2MMEAN | 8.0 MB per month |

- Daily min/max exists as a ready product (M2SDNXSLV). Per NASA docs the daily max/min are saved from the model time step in which they occur.
- Per NASA docs (VERIFIED, read), M2SMNXSLV holds monthly means of daily statistics, so monthly T2MMAX = average of daily highs, T2MMIN = average of daily lows. It is not the single hottest or coldest reading of the month.
- Units K, fill value 9.99999987e+14 (read from the file; stream.py FILL = 1e14 handles it).

## Trend test on day-time, night-time and gap (engine, meaningful = 0.02 K/yr test value, VERIFIED, run locally)

| Series | Verdict | Slope (Theil-Sen) K/yr | 95% range | p-value | Needed for meaningful |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T2MMAX (day) | no_trend | -0.00608 | -0.01285 to +0.00054 | 0.4101 | 42.6 y |
| T2MMIN (night) | detected | +0.01352 | +0.00925 to +0.01780 | 0.0001 | 32.2 y |
| Gap (max minus min) | detected | -0.01923 | -0.02545 to -0.01298 | 0.0041 | 39.6 y |

Predicted vs measured: night slope in +0.003 to +0.015 and larger than day (Match); day slope -0.005 to +0.008 (Miss, -0.0061); day not significant (Match); night p-value 0.02 to 0.3 (Miss, 0.0001); gap negative (Match).

Reading: nights warm about 0.62 K over 45.9 years, days do not change significantly, so the day-night gap narrows by about 0.9 K. The mean temperature (T2M, p = 0.24) hides this split. The OLS slopes add up exactly (-0.00573 - 0.01338 = -0.01911, the gap OLS slope). The gap is derived from the other two series, so it is not an independent finding.
Cautions: one box only; MERRA-2 is a reanalysis, not station data; 0.0135 K/yr is below the 0.02 test value for "meaningful"; the day-time verdict is close to flipping (needs 42.6 of 45.9 years).
